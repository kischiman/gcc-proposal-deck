Suggested rates on the public board Decide line by line, using the suggest control on each. A line that is off gives collaborators no anchor and never reveals its base rate.
Budget visible on the public board Off by default. Anyone with the public link sees scope only — quantities, units, and who is on each line. Turn it on to publish rates, amounts and totals. Collaborators can still enter their own rate either way; it just is not shown to anyone else while this is off.
Proposals visible on the public board Off by default. Everyone would see who has put themselves forward for each line and what they wrote. The lines take proposals either way — this only decides whether the public deck shows them back.
Public comments visible on the deck On by default. A comment left on the deck is public and shows there; one left on the team page never does, whatever this says. Turn this off to take the public threads down without losing them — you still read every comment here and on the team page.
