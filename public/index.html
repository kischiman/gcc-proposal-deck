Asilo · General Construction Co.

01 · Overview

Make the losses visible before they become costs.

GCC has seen small inefficiencies turn into real costs. Most stay invisible until it is too late: nobody has measured where they start, what each one costs, or what they add up to across the company. This work brings them into view and starts closing them.

01

Unmeasured inefficiency

Losses and lost profit are felt across the company but never sized, so nobody can say what to fix first.

02

Costly mistakes

The recent pricing error on a tender and award is one example. Until it's clear where mistakes cluster, the same kind can happen again, and cost more as projects get bigger.

03

Uneven departments

Some teams draw people in and others don't. The reason, often how a team is led, stays hidden until someone leaves.

04

Routine work without AI

There's no shared guideline on which admin, finance and accounts tasks AI should take on, or with which tools. Construction-specific software stays with specialists.

Human coordination first, then technical implementation.

Costs start where people hand work to each other. We find those points in the department, close them before they become costs, and then automate what is left.
